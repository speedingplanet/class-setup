# A Note On AI

As of this writing (January 2026), artificial intelligence (AI) has transformed the world of computer programming. This document aims to stake out a position on using AI in Speeding Planet's classes, generally. 

If a primary subject of the class is AI, of course you should use it in class. Obviously. Otherwise....

## Use of AI in the classroom is strongly discouraged

The two main points of any given class are to learn 1) about a subject and 2) how to work with that subject. AI has negative effects on both these goals. There are multiple studies indicating that use of AI while learning is devastating to both apprehension and retention of information.  

That said, you are an adult and can make your own adult decisions. The instructor will not comment on your use of AI, negatively or positively. There will be no rants in class, pro or con.
